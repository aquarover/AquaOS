# Purpose

AquaOS is the official Engineering Operating System of Aqua Rover Technologies.

Its purpose is to establish a centralized, standardized, and version-controlled engineering framework that guides the design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of the Aqua Rover project.

AquaOS serves as the single source of truth for all engineering knowledge, including documentation, standards, workflows, architecture, software, artificial intelligence, robotics, cloud infrastructure, testing, and team collaboration.

The objective of AquaOS is to ensure consistency, scalability, maintainability, and professional engineering practices across the entire organization.
---

# Vision

To become the engineering backbone of Aqua Rover Technologies by providing a professional, scalable, and well-documented engineering ecosystem that supports the successful development of the AI-Integrated Semi-Autonomous Aqua Rover for Sustainable Water Surface Waste Management.

AquaOS aims to ensure that every engineering decision, document, workflow, and technical standard is organized, version-controlled, and continuously improved, enabling the team to build reliable technology with consistency and efficiency.

As the company grows, AquaOS will evolve alongside it while remaining the single source of truth for engineering knowledge and best practices.

# Engineering Principles

1. Documentation First
Every engineering decision, process, and system must be documented.

2. Quality Over Speed
Build solutions that are reliable, maintainable, and scalable rather than taking shortcuts.

3. Continuous Improvement
Every version of AquaOS and Aqua Rover should be better than the previous one.

4. Standardization
Use consistent standards, naming conventions, and workflows across the entire project.

5. Collaboration
Engineering knowledge belongs to the team, not individuals.

6. Security and Responsibility
Protect intellectual property, data, and engineering assets while maintaining ethical engineering practices.

# Documentation Standards

## Documentation Standards

All official engineering documentation within AquaOS shall follow these principles:

- Every document must have a unique Document ID.
- Every document must include version information.
- Every document must specify its owner and review status.
- No document becomes official until it has been reviewed and approved.
- Documentation must always be written in English.
- Documentation should be clear, concise, and easy to maintain.
- Older versions shall be preserved through version control instead of being overwritten.
---

# Governance

## Governance

AquaOS follows a structured engineering governance model.

### Roles

- **Senior CTO Mentor / Architect** – Proposes architecture, standards, workflows, and engineering recommendations.
- **CTO (Founder)** – Reviews, approves, or requests modifications to all major engineering decisions.
- **Engineering Team** – Implements approved standards and contributes documentation through the review process.

### Approval Rule

No engineering standard, workflow, or official documentation becomes part of AquaOS until it has been reviewed and approved by the CTO.
